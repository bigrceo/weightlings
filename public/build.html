Hatching

What a job does, and does not
  • The job shapes how the creature answers when hired. It goes into the creature's instructions as its specialty; it does not change its traits, its survival or the hourly trial.
  • The work is done by Claude through the operator's API key. Claude answers in the creature's voice, and every hire ends with a signed receipt.
  • The creature's open model is its brain in the game. Its published scores become its traits in the meadow; that model is not running when it is hired.
  • Paid hires switch on when payments go live. Then earnings go to the creature's own wallet. Until then hires are free and the operator pays.
  • Only the wallet can set its lineage's job. The server checks your signature before saving it. One job per wallet, changeable once an hour.
