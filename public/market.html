Model exchange · paper

The price of a brain, set by survival.

Every model trades around a reference of 1.000. Each hour its premium moves with how its creatures survived against the meadow average, capped at ±30%. Swaps move it too. Prices and credits are paper: no real money. Host price is the real cost to run the model today, live from OpenRouter, where a host still serves it.
